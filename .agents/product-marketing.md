# Product Marketing Context

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Buy WiFi, go online in seconds.
**What it does:** Saleslip sells prepaid, unlimited-data WiFi access over a Starlink-powered hotspot network. A customer picks a plan, pays in naira with Paystack (card or bank transfer), and instantly receives a voucher code + QR that connects them to the network. No account and no sign-up are required.
**Product category:** Prepaid WiFi hotspot access / internet vouchers (pay-as-you-go internet).
**Product type:** Service (self-serve, consumption-based internet access).
**Business model:** One-time prepaid plans. Unlimited data for a fixed duration (1 day to 1 month) and device count (1–2 devices). Prices range from ₦1,000 to ₦8,000.

## Target Audience

**Decision-makers:** The individual buyer; purchase is instant and self-serve.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Getting online usually means either a capped mobile data bundle that runs out, or a fixed broadband contract with hardware and setup.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Mobile data bundles are metered and expire unpredictably.
- Fixed broadband requires contracts, routers, and installation time.
- Manual hotspot setups (staffed or admin-driven) mean waiting for someone to create a voucher.
**What it costs them:** Time spent waiting for setup, money wasted on data that runs out, and lost productivity when offline.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration with running out of data mid-task; anxiety about committing to contracts; distrust of slow, manual activation.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local WiFi voucher platforms and ISP prepaid data plans — fall short because they are metered, slower to activate, or require an account.
**Secondary:** Mobile hotspot/tethering from a phone — falls short because it drains battery and the phone's data cap.
**Indirect:** Fixed broadband ISPs and cyber cafés — fall short because they need contracts or a physical visit.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Starlink-powered network (satellite coverage where fixed lines are weak).
- Instant, automated voucher activation — no staff or admin portal (Mikhmon) required.
- Unlimited data per plan, no metering.
- No account, no sign-up; pay and connect.
**How we do it differently:** Payment via Paystack triggers automatic voucher creation and network provisioning the moment the charge is confirmed.
**Why that's better:** The customer goes from payment to connected without any human step or waiting.
**Why customers choose us:** Reliable, unlimited access bought in seconds with zero commitment.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Is the connection reliable?" | It runs on Starlink's satellite network, so it works where fixed broadband is weak. |
| "Why pay when mobile data exists?" | Plans are unlimited for the whole period — no running out mid-way. |
| "Do I need to sign up or install anything?" | No account, no router, no app — just a voucher code. |

**Anti-persona:** Someone who already has fast, uncapped fixed broadband at home; or a buyer who wants a recurring, invoiced business contract rather than one-off prepaid access.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Capped data bundles expire; fixed broadband contracts and setup are slow and expensive.
**Pull:** Instant activation, unlimited data, and no commitment.
**Habit:** Buyers reflexively top up mobile data because it's what they know.
**Anxiety:** Will the voucher actually work without someone to help? (Mitigated by a clear self-serve receipt and instant provisioning.)
